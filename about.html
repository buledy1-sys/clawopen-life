The Experiment Framework

Based on Kyle MacDonald's "One Red Paperclip" (2005-2006)

Kyle, unemployed at 26, traded a single red paperclip for a house in exactly 14 trades over 1 year. His key insight: value is subjective — story value beats market value.

His trades:

  1. Red paperclip → Fish-shaped pen
  2. Fish pen → Hand-sculpted doorknob
  3. Doorknob → Coleman camping stove
  4. Stove → Honda generator
  5. Generator → "Instant party" kit (keg + neon sign + beer IOU)
  6. Party kit → Ski-Doo snowmobile
  7. Snowmobile → Trip to Yahk, BC
  8. Trip → Cube van
  9. Van → Recording contract (Ontario studio)
  10. Recording contract → 1 year's rent in Phoenix
  11. Rent → Afternoon with Alice Cooper
  12. Alice Cooper → Motorized KISS snow globe
  13. Snow globe → Paid role in Corbin Bernsen movie
  14. Movie role → 2-story farmhouse in Kipling, Saskatchewan

His success factors:

  • Personal blog documenting every trade
  • Used Craigslist barter section (no Reddit — it didn't exist yet)
  • Traveled to meet everyone in person
  • Media attention grew with each trade
  • Final trade was the town of Kipling (wanted PR)
  • Timeline: exactly 1 year

Our Version (2026)

Differences from Kyle's Experiment

  • AI makes every decision — Not me. AI analyzes offers, scores opportunities, drafts pitches
  • Digital platforms — Reddit r/SomethingWorthTrading, TikTok, Instagram (not Craigslist)
  • Full transparency — Every decision published before I act
  • Audience participation — TikTok followers help find trades
  • Faster pace — Max 48 hours per trade (vs. Kyle's variable timeline)

Our Rules

  1. Never trade for cash — always trade UP for an item
  2. Look for asymmetric value (story > market price)
  3. Trade online, meet in person (safety first)
  4. Publish everything on clawopen.life before action
  5. Post every step on TikTok — audience = leverage
  6. Zero financial commitment — I invest time only
  7. Max 48 hours per trade — pivot if stalled
