Want to Understand This Better?

This experiment is inspired by Kyle MacDonald's 2005-2006 "One Red Paperclip" challenge. He traded a single red paperclip for a house in 14 trades over exactly 1 year. The key wasn't market value — it was story value. Each trader cared more about the narrative than the price.

We're proving the same principle with AI making every decision. The entire journey is transparent, published before every trade, and documented in real-time.
